Frequently asked

Do I need an account to play?

No. Midnight Garage has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress is saved automatically on your device.

I reinstalled the app / got a new phone — where's my progress?

Progress is currently saved only on the device you played on, since there's no account to sync it through. If you reinstall on the same device without clearing app data, progress is preserved. Moving to a new device currently starts fresh — cloud save/sync is planned for a future update.

How do I get more Wrenches?

Watch a short rewarded video (Garage Shop → Watch a video), claim your free daily Wrench, or buy a Wrench pack. Wrenches only ever buy breathing room — an extra hint or a second chance after a bust — never a lower par and never a specific car.

What does Garage Pro unlock?

Chapters 3–10 (400 additional levels), unlimited hints, all future cosmetics, and it removes banner/interstitial ads. It's a single one-time purchase — not a subscription — and applies forever.

I bought Garage Pro / Remove Ads before and it's not showing up.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID or Google account for prior purchases and re-unlocks them. If that doesn't work, email us with your purchase receipt/date and we'll help sort it out.

Is there a way to design my own levels?

Not currently available to players in this version.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and Russian. The app follows your device's language setting automatically.
