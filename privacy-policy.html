The short version

Midnight Garage does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to identify you as an individual. Your game progress is saved only on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

What the app stores on your device

Your level progress, streaks, settings, and any purchases you've made are saved locally on your device using standard platform storage (iOS UserDefaults / Android SharedPreferences, via the Capacitor Preferences plugin). This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to any server we operate, and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email address, or any account information — the app has no accounts.
  • No location data.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos — the app never requests these permissions.
  • No analytics are active in this version of the app. (The app's code includes an optional, privacy-preserving analytics system — a randomly generated device identifier plus anonymous gameplay facts, never linked to any personal information — but it is switched off in this build and sends nothing. Advertising

Midnight Garage shows ads via Google AdMob, unless you own the "Remove Ads" or "Pro Garage" unlock, in which case banner and interstitial ads are turned off entirely (a separate "Watch a video" option to earn in-game currency stays available to everyone, ad-supported or not, and is never shown without you tapping it first).

By default, ads are non-personalized: no advertising identifier is used to track you across apps or websites. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and UK), you'll be shown a consent form; if you choose to allow personalized ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) to show ads tailored to you. On iOS, personalized ads additionally require you to approve the system App Tracking Transparency prompt — declining it, or declining the consent form, keeps ads non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in the app's ad privacy settings.

See Google's own privacy policy for how AdMob itself handles data: policies.google.com/privacy, and its ad-specific disclosures at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

In-app purchases

Midnight Garage offers optional one-time purchases (a "Pro Garage" unlock and optional "Wrenches" currency packs). All payment processing is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation from Apple/Google that a purchase was completed, so the app can unlock the corresponding content on your device.

Children's privacy

Midnight Garage does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since the app has no accounts and no data collection, there is no personal information to collect regardless of a user's age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds optional analytics or advertising — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and the change will be reflected in that version's App Store / Play Store release notes.
